OurWorld — logo proposals

Three directions for RF16, each at 512, 192, 32 and 16 px, on paper and on night, in colour and in one colour. Colours come only from the design tokens: personal pink #D9467A and teal #2F8F83 (the two of us, as in today's logo), the "new" yellow #F7D774, and the night sky for the app icon. Nothing is wired into the app yet: pick one and the rest follows.
